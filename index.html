About

I am a Research Economist at the Banco de España — Financial Stability and Macroprudential Policy Department. I received my PhD in Economics from Universitat Pompeu Fabra.

My research examines how financial intermediaries shape credit supply and real economic outcomes. I study how banks’ sectoral specialization affects monetary policy transmission, how collateral valuation uncertainty generates frictions in mortgage markets, and how lender incentives interact with firm dynamics and resource allocation.

My work combines granular administrative datasets — including credit registers, mortgage application records, and firm-level data — with structural and reduced-form methods to identify causal mechanisms and quantify their aggregate implications.

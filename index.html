About

I am a Research Economist in the Financial Stability and Macroprudential Policy Department at the Banco de España. I received my PhD in Economics from Universitat Pompeu Fabra.

My research examines how financial intermediaries shape the allocation and transmission of credit. I focus on how lender heterogeneity affects the distribution of credit across firms and households, with implications for monetary policy, financial stability, and aggregate productivity. I study these questions in corporate and mortgage credit markets, focusing on bank specialization, collateral valuation uncertainty, and firm credit dynamics.

I combine granular administrative data—including credit registers, mortgage application records, and firm-level data—with structural and reduced-form methods.
